What is Nerdtown Games?

Nerdtown Games is a free collection of browser games you can play instantly. Everything runs in your browser, so there is nothing to download or install, and no account is needed to start playing.

The site is built to have no ads and to load quickly on school computers, work machines, and phones. Games are built with HTML5, so they run on most modern browsers.

What you'll find on the site

  • Quick arcade and puzzle games for short breaks
  • Action, racing, and platformer games
  • Strategy and idle games for longer sessions
  • Two-player games you can play on one keyboard

Why are there several links?

Some schools and workplaces filter websites by address. Because Nerdtown Games is available on more than one address, if your network blocks one link you can usually open another. All links lead to the same games.

Frequently asked questions

A link says "Unavailable". Does that mean it's down?

Not necessarily. The status is checked from your own network, so a link marked "Unavailable" may only be blocked where you are. You can still try the Play button, or pick a different link.

None of the links open. What can I do?

Try again later, switch to a different network such as mobile data if you're allowed to, or email us and we'll look into it.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Games run directly in your browser.

How do I report a broken game or a blocked link?

Email games.nerdtown@gmail.com with the game name or link and what happened.
